### Tensile Stress and Tensile Strain

*Source slides 5–6.*

- There are two terms for the behaviour of material undergoing tensile forces: tensile [[Glossary#Stress|stress]] and tensile [[Glossary#Strain|strain]].
- Tensile stress is defined as the [[Glossary#Force|force]] applied per unit area.
- Stress is increased if you stand on a trampoline rather than lying down.
- Tensile strain is defined as the fractional change in the original length of the wire.

### Stress-Strain Graph for a Metal

*Source slide 7.*

- This graph will show a stress-strain curve for a ductile material.
- A ductile material can be easily shaped.
- UTS: Ultimate tensile stress that the material can withstand before it breaks.
- Between these yield points the material rapidly extends.
- A strong material is one with a large UTS.

### The Young Modulus, E

*Source slides 8–9.*

- Before the limit of proportionality is passed stress is directly proportional to strain.
- [[Glossary#Young Modulus|Young modulus]] is the gradient of the linear region of the stress-strain graph.
- A material is a high Young modulus is stiff.

### More Stress-Strain Graphs

*Source slides 12–14.*

- Not all materials respond in the same way.
- You can infer a lot about materials from their characteristic stress-strain graphs.
- A brittle material will exhibit elastic behaviour until their braking point.
- This is for materials such as glass and cast iron.
- Polymeric materials consist of long molecular chains.
- Rubber is a type of polymeric material and shows elastic properties.
- Polythene is also a polymeric material.

## Equations

### Stress, strain and Young modulus

$$\sigma=\frac{F}{A}$$

Tensile stress.

$$\varepsilon=\frac{\Delta L}{L}$$

Tensile strain.

$$E=\frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon}$$

Young modulus in the linear elastic region.

## Method

1. Use the original cross-sectional area and original length unless told otherwise.
2. Determine Young modulus from the gradient of a stress-strain graph in its straight-line region.

## Common mistakes

- Strain is dimensionless; stress and Young modulus have unit pascal.
