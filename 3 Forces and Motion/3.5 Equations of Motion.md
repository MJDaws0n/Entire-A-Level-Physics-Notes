### Deriving Equations of Motion

*Source slides 16–20.*

- Let’s start by stating what we know.
- We know that the area under a v-t graph is the [[Glossary#Displacement|displacement]], s.
- Area is the displacement, s.
- We will use [1] and [3] to find another relationship by eliminating time, t.

## Equations

### Uniform acceleration equations

$$v=u+at$$

Final velocity after time \(t\).

$$s=\frac{1}{2}(u+v)t$$

Displacement from mean velocity.

$$s=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2$$

Displacement without final velocity.

$$v^2=u^2+2as$$

Motion without time.

See [[Formula Sheet]] for the complete course formula set.

## Method

1. Write down \(u,v,a,s,t\) with signs and units.
2. Select an equation containing the known quantities and the one unknown.
3. These equations apply only while acceleration is constant.

## Common mistakes

- Do not automatically make \(g\) negative; its sign follows the chosen positive direction.
