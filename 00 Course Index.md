## Course topics

### [[00 2 Foundations of Physics Index|2 Foundations of Physics]]

Units, dimensional reasoning, scalars, vectors and component methods. (6 lessons)

### [[00 3 Forces and Motion Index|3 Forces and Motion]]

Describing motion with rates, graphs, constant-acceleration equations and projectiles. (9 lessons)

### [[00 4 Forces in action Index|4 Forces in action]]

Free-body diagrams, drag, equilibrium, moments, pressure and upthrust. (9 lessons)

### [[00 5 Work, Energy, and Power Index|5 Work, Energy, and Power]]

Energy transfers, conservation, work, power and efficiency. (4 lessons)

### [[00 6 Materials Index|6 Materials]]

Springs, deformation, elastic energy, stress, strain and Young modulus. (4 lessons)

### [[00 7 Laws of Motion Index|7 Laws of Motion]]

Newton's laws, momentum, impulse and collisions. (5 lessons)

### [[00 8 Charge and Current Index|8 Charge and Current]]

Charge carriers, current, conservation of charge and drift speed. (5 lessons)

### [[00 9 Energy, Power, and Resistance Index|9 Energy, Power, and Resistance]]

Electrical energy, resistance, component characteristics and sensor behaviour. (11 lessons)

### [[00 10 Electrical Circuits Index|10 Electrical Circuits]]

Kirchhoff analysis, resistor networks, internal resistance and potential dividers. (7 lessons)

### [[00 11 Waves 1 Index|11 Waves 1]]

Progressive waves, reflection, refraction, diffraction, polarisation and intensity. (9 lessons)

### [[00 12 Waves 2 Index|12 Waves 2]]

Superposition, interference, stationary waves and harmonics. (6 lessons)

### [[00 13 Quantum Physics Index|13 Quantum Physics]]

Photons, the photoelectric effect and wave-particle duality. (4 lessons)

### [[00 14 Thermal Physics Index|14 Thermal Physics]]

Temperature, states of matter, internal energy, heat capacity and latent heat. (5 lessons)

### [[00 15 Ideal Gas Index|15 Ideal Gas]]

Gas laws, kinetic theory, rms speed and the Boltzmann constant. (4 lessons)

### [[00 16 Circular Motion Index|16 Circular Motion]]

Radians, angular quantities and centripetal acceleration and force. (3 lessons)

### [[00 17 Oscillations Index|17 Oscillations]]

Simple harmonic motion, energy, damping, driving and resonance. (5 lessons)

### [[00 18 Gravitational Fields Index|18 Gravitational Fields]]

Newtonian gravitation, fields, potentials, orbits and satellites. (7 lessons)

### [[00 19 Stars Index|19 Stars]]

Stellar classification, evolution, spectra, luminosity and atomic energy levels. (7 lessons)

### [[00 20 Cosmology Index|20 Cosmology]]

Astronomical distances, redshift, Hubble's law and evolution of the Universe. (5 lessons)

### [[00 21 Capacitance Index|21 Capacitance]]

Capacitor circuits, stored energy and exponential charging and discharging. (6 lessons)

### [[00 22 Electric Fields Index|22 Electric Fields]]

Coulomb forces, field strength, potential and charged-particle motion. (5 lessons)

### [[00 23 Magnetic Fields Index|23 Magnetic Fields]]

Forces in magnetic fields, induction, Faraday and Lenz laws, and transformers. (6 lessons)

### [[00 24 Particle Physics Index|24 Particle Physics]]

Nuclei, particle families, quarks and beta decay. (5 lessons)

### [[00 25 Radioactivity Index|25 Radioactivity]]

Nuclear emissions, decay equations, half-life, activity and dating. (6 lessons)

### [[00 26 Nuclear Physics Index|26 Nuclear Physics]]

Mass-energy, binding energy, fission and fusion. (4 lessons)

### [[00 27 Medical Imaging Index|27 Medical Imaging]]

X-rays, CT, nuclear imaging, ultrasound, impedance and Doppler methods. (8 lessons)
