### Wave-Particle Duality

*Source slide 3.*

- Louis de Broglie was interested in the ideas that Einstein had highlighted about [[Glossary#Wave-Particle Duality|wave-particle duality]] for light.
- De Broglie proposed that all subatomic particles can exhibit [[Glossary#Diffraction|diffraction]] patterns.
- This was experimentally proven with electrons and de Broglie won the Noble Prize in 1929.

## Equations

### Photons and wave-particle duality

$$E=hf=\frac{hc}{\lambda}$$

Photon energy.

$$p=\frac{h}{\lambda}$$

Photon momentum and de Broglie relationship.

$$\lambda=\frac{h}{p}$$

de Broglie wavelength of a particle.

See [[Formula Sheet]] for the complete course formula set.

## Method

1. Convert wavelength to metres before using \(hc/\lambda\).
2. For a massive non-relativistic particle, find momentum from \(p=mv\).

## Common mistakes

- Intensity changes the number of photons per second; frequency changes the energy of each photon.
