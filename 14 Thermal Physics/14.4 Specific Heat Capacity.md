## Core notes

### 14.4 Specific Heat Capacity

*Source slide 1.*

- Increasing the temperature of a substance increases the [[Glossary#Kinetic Energy|kinetic energy]] and therefore the [[Glossary#Internal Energy|internal energy]] of a substance.
- During a phase change there is a change of internal [[Glossary#Energy|energy]] as the potential energy can increase or decrease.

### SHC Definition

*Source slide 3.*

- The energy required to change the temperature by 1 K (or 1 °C) of a 1 kg mass.

### Temperature-time graphs

*Source slide 9.*

- A graph of temperature against time for a substance heated at a constant rate shows a linear relationship.

### Method of Mixtures

*Source slide 10.*

- The method of mixtures is another way of determining the SHC of a material when you already know the SHC of another material.
- For example, if I know the SHC of I can add another other material to water and wait for them to become at thermal equilibrium.
- I can then calculate the SHC of the material.
