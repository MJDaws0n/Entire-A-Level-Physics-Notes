## Core notes

### 14.5 Specific Latent Heat

*Source slide 1.*

- The [[Glossary#Energy|energy]] required to change the temperature by 1 K (or 1 °C) of a 1 kg mass.

### Specific Latent Heat Definition

*Source slide 3.*

- The energy required to change the state of a 1 kg substance with no temperature change.

### Specific Latent Heat

*Source slide 4.*

- Specific Latent Heat (SLH) tells us how much energy is required to change the state of a 1 kg block with no temperature change.
- Specific Latent Heat for vaporisation
- Specific Latent Heat for fusion.

### Experimentally Determining SLH of Fusion

*Source slide 6.*

- You can use a simple electrical heater to melt ice to determine the SLH of fusion for ice.

### Experimentally Determining SLH of Vaporisation

*Source slide 7.*

- Determining the SLH of vaporisation requires, in general, a more complicated apparatus than for SLH of fusion.

## Equations

### Specific latent heat

$$\Delta E=mL$$

Energy for a change of state at constant temperature.

See [[Formula Sheet]] for the complete course formula set.

## Method

1. Use only the mass that changes state.
2. Split a multi-stage process into heating and state-change stages.

## Common mistakes

- Do not use \(\Delta E=mc\Delta\theta\) during the ideal constant-temperature phase change.
