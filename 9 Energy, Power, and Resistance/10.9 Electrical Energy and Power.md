## Core notes

### 9.10 Electrical Energy and Power

*Source slide 1.*

- 1) Convert the written definitions into math formulae.
- a) [[Glossary#Power|Power]] (P) is the rate of [[Glossary#Work Done|work done]] (W) in per units time (t).
- b) [[Glossary#Potential Difference|Potential difference]] (V) is work done (W) per a units charge (Q).

### Electrical Energy and Power

*Source slide 4.*

- The Three Gorges Dam is the largest power plant in the world and is situated in China.
- It has an output of 22.5 GW which is enough power to boils 10 million kettles at once.
- Electricity generated is easy to distribute via the grid and this is one reason that electricity is so ubiquitous.

### Electrical Power

*Source slide 5.*

- The rate of [[Glossary#Energy|energy]] transfer by each electrical component is called the electrical power (P).
- It depends upon the amount of current (I) flowing through the component and potential difference (V) across the component.
- Power is proportional to voltage and current.

## Equations

### Electrical energy and power

$$P=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R}$$

Equivalent power relationships.

$$W=Pt=VIt$$

Electrical energy transferred.

$$\text{cost}=\text{energy in kWh}\times\text{price per kWh}$$

Domestic electricity cost.

### Potential difference and electromotive force

$$V=\frac{W}{Q}$$

Potential difference: energy transferred per unit charge.

$$\mathcal{E}=\frac{W_{\mathrm{source}}}{Q}$$

EMF: energy supplied per unit charge.

$$W=VQ=VIt$$

Electrical energy transferred.

See [[Formula Sheet]] for the complete course formula set.

## Method

1. Choose the power form that uses known quantities.
2. For billing, convert power to kilowatts and time to hours.
3. For SI energy in joules, use power in watts and time in seconds.
4. Identify whether a component supplies energy or transfers it away from the charges.
5. Use joules per coulomb to interpret both volts and EMF.

## Common mistakes

- A kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy, not power.
- EMF is measured in volts; despite its name, it is not a force.
