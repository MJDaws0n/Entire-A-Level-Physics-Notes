### Gold, Gold, Gold!

*Source slides 3–4.*

- Gold is a very conductive material due to its high number [[Glossary#Density|density]] of electrons, n.
- Many people in the unscrupulous world of advertising talk about the benefits of using gold as a material that will increase your viewing or listening pleasure.
- The premium price is because gold is expensive and people associate this with being better.
- The gains are minimal if they exist.
- Take away message: Do not be a magpie.

### Factors Affecting Resistance

*Source slide 5.*

- At this stage we will say that all electrical components have a property called [[Glossary#Resistance|resistance]].
- We know temperature affects resistance.
- As you increase temperature the resistance increases.

### Resistance and length are directly proportional.

*Source slide 7.*

- Increasing the length by a factor of two increases the resistance by a factor of two.
- Resistance and cross-sectional area are inversely proportional.
- For a given [[Glossary#Potential Difference|potential difference]], doubling the cross-sectional area will double the current and thus half the resistance.

### Determining Resistivity

*Source slide 12.*

- We change the length of the wire and calculate the resistance.

### Resistivity

*Source slide 13.*

- Increasing the temperature of a material also increases the [[Glossary#Resistivity|resistivity]].
- Therefore, we need to state the temperature when we state the resistivity of a material.

## Equations

### Resistance and resistivity

$$R=\frac{V}{I}$$

Resistance at a particular operating point.

$$R=\frac{\rho L}{A}$$

Resistance of a uniform conductor.

See [[Formula Sheet]] for the complete course formula set.

## Method

1. For an ohmic conductor, use the gradient of a \(V\)-against-\(I\) graph.
2. In resistivity work, measure \(L\) and diameter carefully and use \(A=\pi d^2/4\).

## Common mistakes

- The reciprocal gradient is required if the graph plots \(I\) against \(V\).
- Resistivity is a material property; resistance also depends on dimensions.
