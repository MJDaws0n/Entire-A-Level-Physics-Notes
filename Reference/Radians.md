# Radians

[[16.1 Angular velocity and the radian|Angular Velocity and the Radian]] · [[Formula Sheet]]

A radian is defined by arc length:

$$\theta=\frac{s}{r}$$

A full circle has \(s=2\pi r\), so:

$$360^{\circ}=2\pi\ \mathrm{rad}$$

## Degrees to radians

$$\theta_{\mathrm{rad}}=\theta_{\mathrm{deg}}\frac{\pi}{180}$$

## Radians to degrees

$$\theta_{\mathrm{deg}}=\theta_{\mathrm{rad}}\frac{180}{\pi}$$

Use radians in \(s=r\theta\), \(v=\omega r\), SHM phase equations and any small-angle approximation.
