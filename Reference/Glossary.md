# Glossary

[[00 Course Index|Course index]] · [[Formula Sheet]]

## A

### Absolute Temperature

Temperature measured from absolute zero on the kelvin scale.

### Acceleration

Rate of change of velocity.

### Acoustic Impedance

Product \(Z=\rho c\), describing how a medium responds to an acoustic wave.

### Activity

Number of nuclear decays per second, measured in becquerels.

### Amplitude

Maximum displacement from equilibrium.

### Antinodes

Points on a stationary wave with maximum oscillation amplitude.

## B

### Baryon

Hadron made from three quarks.

### Binding Energy

Energy needed to separate a nucleus completely into free nucleons.

## C

### Capacitance

Charge stored per unit potential difference.

### Centripetal Acceleration

Acceleration directed toward the centre of circular motion.

### Coherent

Having the same frequency and a constant phase difference.

### Conservation Of Charge

Total electric charge in an isolated system remains constant.

### Conservation Of Energy

Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred between stores.

### Conservation Of Momentum

Total momentum of an isolated system remains constant.

### Critical Angle

Incidence angle in the higher-index medium that produces a refracted angle of \(90^{\circ}\).

### Current

Rate of flow of charge.

## D

### Decay Constant

Probability per unit time that a nucleus decays.

### Density

Mass per unit volume.

### Diffraction

Spreading of a wave after an aperture or around an obstacle.

### Displacement

Directed change in position.

### Drag

Resistive force opposing relative motion through a fluid.

### Drift Velocity

Mean velocity of charge carriers caused by an electric field.

## E

### Efficiency

Useful output divided by total input.

### Elastic Deformation

Deformation that reverses when the force is removed.

### Electric Field Strength

Force per unit positive test charge.

### Electric Potential

Potential energy per unit charge.

### Electromotive Force

Energy supplied by a source per unit charge.

### Energy

Capacity of a system to do work or cause change.

## F

### Field

Region in which an object experiences a force because of a property such as mass or charge.

### Flux Linkage

Product of coil turns and magnetic flux through each turn.

### Force

Interaction that can change an object's momentum.

### Frequency

Number of complete cycles per second.

## G

### Gravitational Field Strength

Force per unit mass.

### Gravitational Potential

Work done per unit mass in bringing a small test mass from infinity.

## H

### Hadron

Particle made from quarks and affected by the strong interaction.

### Half-Life

Time for the number of undecayed nuclei or activity to fall to half its value.

## I

### Impulse

Change in momentum; force multiplied by time for a constant/mean force.

### Intensity

Power transferred per unit area.

### Internal Energy

Sum of random kinetic and intermolecular potential energies of particles.

### Isotope

Atoms with the same proton number but different neutron numbers.

## K

### Kinetic Energy

Energy associated with motion.

## L

### Lepton

Fundamental particle family including electrons and neutrinos.

### Luminosity

Total power radiated by a star.

## M

### Magnetic Flux

Measure \(\Phi=BA\cos\theta\) of magnetic field through an area.

### Mass Defect

Difference between the mass of separated nucleons and the mass of the bound nucleus.

### Moment

Turning effect of a force about a point.

### Momentum

Vector quantity \(p=mv\).

## N

### Node

Point on a stationary wave with zero displacement amplitude.

### Normal

Line perpendicular to a boundary at the point of incidence.

### Nucleon

Proton or neutron.

## P

### Potential Difference

Energy transferred per unit charge between two points.

### Power

Rate of energy transfer or work done.

### Pressure

Normal force per unit area.

### Progressive Wave

Travelling disturbance that transfers energy without net transfer of matter.

## R

### Radian

Angle subtended at a circle's centre by an arc equal in length to the radius.

### Refractive Index

Ratio of light speed in vacuum to light speed in a medium.

### Resistance

Potential difference divided by current at an operating point.

### Resistivity

Material property linking resistance to conductor length and area.

### Resonance

Large-amplitude response when driving frequency is near natural frequency.

## S

### Scalar

Quantity with magnitude only.

### Simple Harmonic Motion

Oscillation with acceleration proportional and opposite to displacement.

### Stationary Wave

Fixed interference pattern formed by equal-frequency waves travelling in opposite directions.

### Strain

Extension divided by original length.

### Stress

Force per unit cross-sectional area.

### Superposition

Resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of individual displacements.

## T

### Terminal Velocity

Constant velocity reached when resultant force becomes zero.

### Time Constant

For an RC circuit, \(\tau=RC\); characteristic time for an exponential change.

### Torque

Turning effect of a couple.

### Total Internal Reflection

Complete reflection at a boundary when travelling from higher to lower index above the critical angle.

## U

### Upthrust

Upward fluid force equal to the weight of displaced fluid.

## V

### Vector

Quantity with magnitude and direction.

### Velocity

Rate of change of displacement.

## W

### Wave-Particle Duality

Quantum objects show both wave-like and particle-like behaviour.

### Wavelength

Shortest distance between two points in the same phase.

### Work Done

Energy transferred by a force acting through a displacement.

### Work Function

Minimum energy required to release an electron from a material surface.

## Y

### Young Modulus

Stress divided by strain in the linear elastic region.
