# Equation Solving Method

[[00 Course Index|Course index]] · [[Formula Sheet]] · [[SI Units, Prefixes and Significant Figures]]

Use the same reliable routine used throughout the lesson PowerPoints.

1. **State what you know.** Write each quantity with its symbol, value and unit.
2. **Convert to SI.** Replace prefixes and convert lengths, areas, volumes and times.
3. **State the equation.** Choose one containing the known values and required unknown.
4. **Rearrange first.** Make the unknown the subject before entering numbers.
5. **Substitute with units.** This makes unit errors visible.
6. **Calculate without early rounding.**
7. **Check.** Ask whether the sign, magnitude, unit and direction are sensible.
8. **Round once.** Match the least precise supplied data unless the question says otherwise.

> [!example] Compact layout
> Given: \(m=2.0\,\mathrm{kg}\), \(a=3.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)  
> Equation: \(F=ma\)  
> Substitute: \(F=(2.0)(3.0)=6.0\,\mathrm{N}\)
