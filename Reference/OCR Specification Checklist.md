# OCR Specification Checklist

[[00 Course Index|Course index]] · [[Content Coverage Audit]] · [[OCR Formula Booklet]]

This is a learner-friendly, paraphrased map of the current OCR Physics A H556 course. Each item points to the lesson or reference note that teaches it. Use the official specification for the legal wording; use this page to navigate the vault.

## Module 1 — Development of practical skills

- [ ] Plan investigations: identify variables, choose equipment and ranges, control variables, and write a repeatable method. See [[Practical Endorsement Lesson]].
- [ ] Carry out work safely and accurately, including risk assessment and suitable apparatus techniques. See [[Practical Endorsement Lesson]].
- [ ] Record raw data with headings, units, precision and repeated readings. See [[Practical Skills and Uncertainties]].
- [ ] Process data with graphs, gradients, intercepts, uncertainty bars and worst acceptable lines. See [[Graphs, Gradients and Data Skills]] and [[Practical Skills and Uncertainties]].
- [ ] Evaluate limitations, distinguish random from systematic effects, and justify realistic improvements. See [[Practical Skills and Uncertainties]].
- [ ] Apply practical skills in unfamiliar contexts and understand the required practical-endorsement techniques. See [[Practical Endorsement Lesson]].

## Module 2 — Foundations of physics

- [ ] Physical quantities, SI base and derived units, prefixes, homogeneity and estimation. See [[2.1 Quantities and Units]], [[2.2 Derived Units]] and [[SI Units, Prefixes and Significant Figures]].
- [ ] Absolute and percentage uncertainty, accuracy, precision, resolution, random/systematic/zero error and uncertainty propagation. See [[Practical Skills and Uncertainties]].
- [ ] Scalars and vectors; scale diagrams, vector addition, resolving, components and equilibrium. See [[2.3 Scalars and Vectors]], [[2.4 Adding Vectors]], [[2.5 Resolving Vectors]] and [[2.6 More Vectors]].

## Module 3 — Forces and motion

- [ ] Distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration and motion graphs. See [[3.1 Distance and Speed]], [[3.2 Displacement and Velocity]], [[3.3 Acceleration]] and [[3.4 More Velocity-Time Graphs]].
- [ ] Constant-acceleration equations, free fall, \(g\), stopping distance and projectile motion. See [[3.5 Equations of Motion]], [[3.6 Stopping Distances]], [[3.7 Free fall and g]] and [[3.8 Projectile Motion]].
- [ ] Force, mass, weight, centre of mass, free-body diagrams, drag and terminal speed. See [[4.1 Force, mass, and weight]], [[4.2 Center of Mass]], [[4.3 Free Body Diagrams]] and [[4.4 Drag and Terminal Velocity]].
- [ ] Moments, couples, torque, equilibrium, triangle of forces, density, pressure and upthrust. See [[4.5 Moments and Equilibrium]], [[4.6 Couples and Torques]], [[4.7 Triangles of Forces]], [[4.8 Density and Pressure]] and [[4.9 Archimedes' Principle]].
- [ ] Work, energy transfers, conservation, kinetic/gravitational energy, power and efficiency. See [[5.1 Work Done and Energy]], [[5.2 Conservation of Energy]], [[5.3 Kinetic and Gravitational Energy]] and [[5.4 Power and Efficiency]].
- [ ] Springs, force-extension graphs, elastic energy, stress, strain and Young modulus. See [[6.1 Springs and Hooke's Law]], [[6.2 Elastic Potential Energy]], [[6.3 Deforming Materials]] and [[6.4 Stress, Strain and Young's Modulus]].
- [ ] Newton's laws, momentum, impulse, force-time graphs and one-/two-dimensional collisions. See [[7.1 Newton's first and third laws]], [[7.2 Linear Momentum]], [[7.3 Newton's second law]], [[7.4 Impulse]] and [[7.5 Collisions in 2D]].

## Module 4 — Electrons, waves and photons

- [ ] Charge, current, charge carriers, Kirchhoff's first law and mean drift velocity. See [[8.1 Charge and Current]], [[8.2 Moving Charges]], [[8.3 Kirchhoff's First Law]] and [[8.4 Mean Drift Velocity]].
- [ ] Circuit symbols, potential difference, EMF, electron acceleration, resistance, I–V characteristics and resistivity. See [[9.1 Circuit Symbols]], [[9.2 Potential Difference and Electromotive Force]], [[9.3 The Electron Gun]], [[9.4 Resisitance]], [[9.5 I-V Characteristics]] and [[9.7 Resistance and Resistivity]].
- [ ] Thermistors, LDRs, electrical energy/power and sensor behaviour. See [[9.8 The Thermistor]], [[9.9 The LDR]], [[10.9 Electrical Energy and Power]] and [[11.9 Paying for Electricity]].
- [ ] Series/parallel circuits, Kirchhoff's laws, internal resistance, terminal p.d., lost volts and potential dividers with sensors. See [[10.1 Kirchhoff's Laws and Circuits]], [[10.2 Combining Resistors]], [[10.3 Analysing Circuits]], [[10.4 Internal Resistance]] and [[10.5 Potential Divider Circuit]].
- [ ] Progressive transverse/longitudinal waves, amplitude, phase, wavelength, frequency, wave speed, intensity and graphical representation. See [[11.1 Progressive Waves]], [[11.2 Wave Properties]], [[11.3 Reflection and Refraction]], [[11.4 Diffraction and Polarisation]] and [[11.5 Intensity]].
- [ ] Electromagnetic spectrum, polarisation, refraction, refractive index, critical angle, total internal reflection and diffraction. See [[11.6 Electromagnetic Waves]], [[11.7 Polarisation Recap]], [[11.8 Refractive Index]], [[11.9 Total Internal Reflection]] and [[11.4 Diffraction and Polarisation]].
- [ ] Superposition, coherence, constructive/destructive interference, Young double slit and diffraction gratings. See [[12.1 Superposition of Waves]], [[12.2 Interference]], [[12.3 Double Slit Experiment]] and [[19.5 Spectra]].
- [ ] Stationary waves, nodes/antinodes, strings, open/closed air columns, fundamental and harmonics. See [[12.4 Stationary Waves]], [[12.5 Harmonics]] and [[12.6 Stationary Waves in Air Columns]].
- [ ] Photons, electronvolts, LEDs and Planck constant. See [[13.1 The Photon Model]].
- [ ] Photoelectric effect, one-photon/one-electron interaction, work function, threshold frequency and intensity/frequency effects. See [[13.2 The Photoelectric Effect]] and [[13.3 The Photoelectric Equation]].
- [ ] Electron diffraction and de Broglie wave-particle duality. See [[13.4 Wave-Particle Duality]].

## Module 5 — Newtonian world and astrophysics

- [ ] Thermal equilibrium, Celsius/kelvin scales, particle models, Brownian motion, internal energy and phase change. See [[14.1 Temperature]], [[14.2 Solids, Liquids and Gases]] and [[14.3 Internal Energy]].
- [ ] Specific heat capacity and specific latent heat, including electrical practical methods. See [[14.4 Specific Heat Capacity]] and [[14.5 Specific Latent Heat]].
- [ ] Moles, Avogadro constant, ideal-gas assumptions, pressure from molecular motion, gas laws and kinetic theory. See [[15.1 Kinetic Theory of Gases]], [[15.2 Gas Laws]] and [[15.3 Root-mean square speed]].
- [ ] Radians, angular speed, centripetal acceleration and centripetal force. See [[16.1 Angular velocity and the radian]], [[16.2 Angular Acceleration]], [[16.3 Centripetal Forces]] and [[Radians]].
- [ ] SHM quantities/equations/graphs, energy exchange, free/forced oscillations, damping and resonance. See [[17.1 Oscillations and SHM]], [[17.2 Analysing SHM]], [[17.3 Energy and SHM]], [[17.4 Damping and Driving]] and [[17.5 Resonance]].
- [ ] Gravitational fields, point/spherical masses, field lines, Newton's gravitation law and field strength. See [[18.1 Gravitational Fields]], [[18.2 Newton's Law of gravitation]] and [[18.3 Gravitational Field Strength]].
- [ ] Kepler's laws, orbital motion, geostationary orbit, gravitational potential/energy and escape speed. See [[18.4 Kepler's Laws]], [[18.5 Satellites]], [[18.6 Gravitational Potential]] and [[18.7 Gravitational Potential Energy]].
- [ ] Stars: energy levels/spectra, Wien and Stefan laws, luminosity/flux, stellar evolution and the Hertzsprung–Russell diagram. See [[19.1 Objects in the Universe]], [[19.2 Life Cycles of Stars]], [[19.3 Hertzsprung-Russell Diagram]], [[19.4 Energy Levels in Atoms]], [[19.6 Analysing Starlight]] and [[19.7 Stellar Luminosity]].
- [ ] Cosmological distances, parallax, standard candles, redshift, Hubble's law, Big Bang evidence and cosmological evolution. See [[20.1 Astronomical Distances]], [[20.2 Doppler Effect]], [[20.3 Hubble's Law]], [[20.4 The Big Bang]] and [[20.5 The Evolution of the Universe]].

## Module 6 — Particles and medical physics

- [ ] Capacitance, \(Q\)-\(V\) graphs, capacitor combinations, stored energy, exponential charging/discharging and uses. See [[21.1 Capacitors]], [[21.2 Capacitors in Circuits]], [[21.3 Energy and Capacitors]], [[21.4 Discharging Capacitors]], [[21.5 Charging Capacitors]] and [[21.6 Use of Capacitors]].
- [ ] Electric fields, Coulomb's law, uniform fields, motion of charged particles, electric potential and potential energy. See [[22.1 Electric Fields]], [[22.2 Coulomb's Law]], [[22.3 Electric Fields and Capacitors]], [[22.4 Charged Particles and Electric Fields]] and [[22.5 Electric Potential and Energy]].
- [ ] Magnetic fields, forces on currents/charges, circular particle motion and velocity selection. See [[23.1 Magnetic Fields]], [[23.2 Understanding Magnetic Fields]] and [[23.3 Charged Particles in Magnetic Fields]].
- [ ] Magnetic flux/linkage, Faraday and Lenz laws, induction, generators and transformers. See [[23.5 Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law]] and [[23.6 Transformers]].
- [ ] Atomic/nuclear structure, alpha scattering, particle families, quarks, conservation laws, interactions and decay equations. See [[24.1 Alpha-particle scattering]], [[24.2 The Nucleus]], [[24.3 Antiparticles, Leptons, and Hadrons]], [[24.4 Quarks]], [[24.5 Beta-decay]] and [[25.2 Nuclear Decay Equations]].
- [ ] Radioactive emissions, background radiation, random/spontaneous decay, activity, half-life, decay constant and exponential modelling/dating. See [[25.1 Radioactivity]], [[25.2 Nuclear Decay Equations]], [[25.3 Half-Life]], [[25.4 Radioactive Decay Calculations]], [[25.5 Modelling Radioactivity]] and [[25.6 Radioactive Dating]].
- [ ] Mass-energy, binding energy, fission, chain reactions/reactors, nuclear waste and fusion. See [[26.1 Mass-Energy Equation]], [[26.2 Binding Energy]], [[26.3 Nuclear Fission]] and [[26.4 Nuclear Fusion]].
- [ ] X-ray production/spectra/attenuation/contrast and CT. See [[27.1 X-rays]], [[27.2 Interaction of X-rays with matter]] and [[27.3 CAT Scans]].
- [ ] Tracers, gamma cameras and PET. See [[27.4 The gamma camera]] and [[27.5 PET Scans]].
- [ ] Ultrasound production/detection, A/B scans, impedance/reflection, matching gel and Doppler blood-flow measurement. See [[27.6 Ultrasound]], [[27.7 Acoustic Impedance]] and [[27.8 Doppler Imaging]].
