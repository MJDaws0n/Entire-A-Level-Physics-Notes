# Practical Skills and Uncertainties

[[Practical Endorsement Lesson]] · [[Graphs, Gradients and Data Skills]] · [[SI Units, Prefixes and Significant Figures]]

## Planning

- State the independent, dependent and controlled variables.
- Give a measurable method, sensible range and enough repeat readings.
- Identify hazards, risks and specific control measures.

## Recording

- Put quantity and unit in each table heading.
- Record raw readings to the precision of the instrument.
- Keep trailing zeros when they communicate measurement precision.

## Uncertainty

For a single reading, use an instrument-based absolute uncertainty. For repeated readings, a useful estimate is:

$$\Delta x=\frac{x_{\max}-x_{\min}}{2}$$

$$\text{percentage uncertainty}=\frac{\Delta x}{x}\times100\%$$

For products and quotients, add percentage uncertainties. For a power \(y=x^n\), the approximate percentage uncertainty in \(y\) is \(|n|\) times that in \(x\).

## Evaluation

A strong evaluation links:

1. a specific limitation;
2. how it changes scatter, bias or the final value;
3. a realistic improvement;
4. why that improvement helps.

Repeats reduce random uncertainty in a mean but do not remove systematic error.
