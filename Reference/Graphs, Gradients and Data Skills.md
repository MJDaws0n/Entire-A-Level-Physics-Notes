# Graphs, Gradients and Data Skills

[[00 Course Index|Course index]] · [[Practical Skills and Uncertainties]]

## Plotting

- Put the independent variable on the horizontal axis unless there is a clear reason not to.
- Label each axis as quantity divided by unit, for example \(t/\mathrm{s}\).
- Use most of the graph area and choose a simple scale.
- Plot small, precise crosses and include uncertainty bars where appropriate.
- Draw a best-fit line or curve; do not join points dot-to-dot.

## Gradient

Choose two well-separated points on the best-fit line, not necessarily raw data points:

$$m=\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$$

Include gradient units. A tangent gives instantaneous gradient on a curve.

## Intercept and linearisation

Compare a transformed relationship with:

$$y=mx+c$$

Examples:

$$x=x_0e^{-t/\tau}\quad\Rightarrow\quad\ln x=\ln x_0-\frac{t}{\tau}$$

$$T^2\propto r^3$$

The gradient and intercept must be interpreted from the rearranged physical equation, including their units.
