### What is the nature of light?

*Source slides 4–5.*

- Isaac Newton was a ground-breaking mathematician and physicist.
- His achievements are numerous.
- Newton co-inventing calculus, brought forward the classical theory of gravity, and showed that white light was a spectrum of colours.
- Newton was a true revolutionary figure with colossal prestige in the scientific community yet this does not mean his ideas shouldn’t be challenged.
- Newton rejected the idea that light was a wave and proposed that light was made of particles which he called corpuscles.
- It took 75 years after Newton’s death for his corpuscular theory of light to be challenged by Thomas Young’s experiment.
- Young’s simple experiment showed that light could produce interference patterns and thus was presumed to be a wave.

### Mathematical Treatment

*Source slides 7–9.*

- Two slits are placed a distance a apart.
- A screen is placed a distance D from the slits.
- A bright fringe is observed at y on the screen.
- Another bright fringe is observed at X.
- The separation between y and X is x.
- Now let us label the angles.
- a is the slit separation

## Equations

### Superposition and interference

$$\Delta x=n\lambda$$

Constructive interference.

$$\Delta x=\left(n+\frac{1}{2}\right)\lambda$$

Destructive interference.

$$\lambda=\frac{ax}{D}$$

Double-slit relationship for small angles.

See [[Formula Sheet]] for the complete course formula set.

## Method

1. Add displacements algebraically at the same place and time.
2. For stable fringes, sources must be coherent: same frequency with constant phase difference.
3. In the double-slit formula, use slit separation \(a\), fringe spacing \(x\), and screen distance \(D\).

## Common mistakes

- Fringe spacing is the distance between adjacent bright fringes, not from the centre to an arbitrary fringe unless divided by fringe number.

> [!note]- Slide 24: Explain the effect on the interference patter has when:
> - A light source with a larger wavelength is used
> - The slit separation is doubled
> - The distance from the slits in increased by a factor of 4.
> - The [[Glossary#Frequency|frequency]] of light is halved
> - x is the distance between the bright fringes.
> - a) As the wavelength increases the distance between the fringes increases.
> - b) As you double the slit separation the bright fringes half in distances.
> - c) As the D increases a factor of 4 the distance between the fringes increase by 4.
> - d) v = f λ, and v = c (constant), so f and λ are inversely proportional.
> - So as frequency is halved, the wavelength is doubled, and x is halved.
